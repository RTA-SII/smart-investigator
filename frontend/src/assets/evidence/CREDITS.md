# Evidence media credits

The files under `rta-1991332-h2f3/` are the evidence shown on case
**RTA-1991332-H2F3** (reckless driving, taxi XD033). They are openly licensed
stock road footage — **not** captures from SMC, and not of the driver named on
that case. No face and no registration plate is legible in any of them.

`trip-recording.webm` is the 480p VP9 transcode Commons publishes, trimmed to
nothing — it is the whole 22-second clip at 2.5 MB, small enough for a Pages
site. **CC BY requires the credit above to travel with it**, so keep this file
alongside the media if you move it, and keep the author line if you swap the
transcode for another.

Replacing any of these with a different file is fine as long as the new one is
openly licensed and this table is updated to match.
